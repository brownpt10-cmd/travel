Island highlights

The Road to Hana, Reimagined

Wind through the surf town of Pāʻia, watch sea turtles sun themselves at Hoʻokipa, and swim beneath Twin Falls before the road opens onto the lava-and-surf drama of Keʻanae. Then sunset dinner at the legendary Mama's Fish House.

Upcountry Escape

Trade sea breezes for cool mountain air. Stroll botanical gardens and lavender fields, taste fresh chèvre and estate wine, and linger on the porch of a historic cattle ranch on the slopes of Haleakalā.

Where Lava Meets the Sea

Just twenty minutes south, La Pérouse Bay unfolds in black lava and turquoise water. Walk the ancient King's Trail at first light, when the coast is golden and quiet.

Roy's-Style Dining · Closest First
In the 1990s, a dozen visionary chefs, Roy Yamaguchi, Bev Gannon and Peter Merriman among them, invented Hawaii Regional Cuisine: island-caught fish, Asian technique and farm-fresh Maui produce. Their kitchens are still some of the island's finest, and several sit just minutes from Wailea.

Book This Week

Getting Around
Rental car

Essential. Check whether your contract excludes the Kaupō backside or the Kahekili Hwy.

Walking

Wailea Beach Path (1.5 mi paved) connects Andaz, the Marriott, Grand Wailea, Four Seasons and Fairmont. Shops at Wailea are a 10-min walk.

Uber / Lyft

Fine around Wailea and Kihei. Scarce Upcountry and on the Hana and Kahekili roads, so don't plan a ride back from there.

Driving tips

Upcountry roads are slower than the map says. Gas up in Kahului or Kihei before Hana, Kahekili or Kaupō.
