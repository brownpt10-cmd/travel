Walk both ways

Hotel → Isartor → Marienplatz → Hotel

Out of the hotel, over the Ludwigsbrücke, through Isartor into the old town, and back the same way. Uses most of the 3-mile budget just getting there and back — the option if you'd rather skip a tram ticket before coffee.

Before You Go

Built around the ankle

Both routes stay under 3 miles and skip every tower climb (Frauenkirche, St. Peter's, Neues Rathaus) — street level only. Old-town cobblestones are mostly flat but uneven in patches; the 20-min-mile pace has slack built in, so there's no need to push through it.

  • Confirm Rischart Café am Marienplatz's hours the morning of — not well documented online. The bakery counter tends to open earlier than sit-down café service.
  • Layer up — late-September mornings in Munich run cool, often near 50°F/10°C before sunrise.
  • For Option B: a single MVV ticket (or the MVGO app) covers the tram or S-Bahn hop — no day pass needed for one ride.
