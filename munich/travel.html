Getting Around

Airport → Hotel ~35 min
Outbound · Mon 9/21

At MUC, follow the blue "S" signs down to the Flughafen München S-Bahn station. Board the S8 toward Herrsching — every 20 min, all day. Buy an Airport–City ticket (~€13, all zones) before boarding. Ride 8 stops (~35 min), no transfer, to Rosenheimer Platz. 4-minute walk to JAMS.

Return · Thu 9/24

Reverse the route: Rosenheimer Platz → S8 → MUC Airport, same ~35 min. Checkout by 1:00 PM — leave the hotel by 1:00–1:15 PM for a comfortable 2.5-hour buffer before the 4:10 PM departure.

Hotel → Hofbräuhaus ~10 min
Outbound · Mon 9/21

Walk 4 minutes to Rosenheimer Platz station. Any S-Bahn (S1–S8 share the same central tunnel) two stops to Marienplatz. From there a 5-minute walk northeast to Hofbräuhaus, Am Platzl 9.

Return

Same route reversed: Marienplatz → any S-Bahn → Rosenheimer Platz. Regular service runs until about 1:20 AM on weeknights, then hourly night trains.

Hotel → Oktoberfest ~15 min
Outbound · Tue 9/22

Walk to Rosenheimer Platz, board any westbound S-Bahn (S1–S8) toward Pasing/Herrsching, ~4 stops (~10 min) to Hackerbrücke. 10-minute walk to the festival's north entrance. To be at the tent door by 9:45 AM, leave the hotel by 9:00 AM.

Return

Hackerbrücke → S-Bahn → Rosenheimer Platz. Trains every 2–5 min during the festival, but Hackerbrücke gets crowded at tent closing (10:30 PM weekdays). Poccistraße or Goetheplatz (U3/U6) are quieter walk-out alternatives if it's mobbed.

Hotel → Garmisch Day Trip ~1h50 total
Outbound · Wed 9/23

Walk to Rosenheimer Platz, S-Bahn to München Hauptbahnhof (~15–20 min). Regional train (RB/RE Werdenfelsbahn, direct) to Garmisch-Partenkirchen, ~90 min. A Bayern-Ticket (~€29) covers the regional fare and local buses for the day — confirm exact times on DB Navigator the day before.

Return

Train back to München Hauptbahnhof, then any S-Bahn to Rosenheimer Platz (~8 min) — full detail on the Wednesday itinerary page.
