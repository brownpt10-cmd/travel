Wed · Day TripSep 23

Zugspitze · Partenkirchen · Ehrenberg (AT)

Rental car, and over the border into Tirol

Same early summit, but a car instead of the train — which buys a long lunch in Partenkirchen and an afternoon at Burgruine Ehrenberg and the highline179 suspension bridge above Reutte, Austria. No gorge.

Heads up: this version does not get you back for a Wednesday-evening tent session. Expect Munich around 8:45 PM, later if you take the scenic route home.

5:40 AM
Walk to Sixt, Munich Ostbahnhof
Haager Str. 3 — ~15-min walk from JAMS, or one S-Bahn stop. Desk opens 6:00 AM.
6:00 AM
Pick up the rental car
Photograph all four corners first. Tell the desk you're crossing into Austria.
6:20 AM
Drive to the Eibsee
Mittlerer Ring → A95 → B2 → Grainau. ~100 km, ~1h 20m at this hour.
~7:45 AM
Park at Seilbahn Zugspitze
Am Eibsee 6, Grainau. Before 8 AM is an easy space — the lot fills and the access road jams by 10.
8:15 AM
First cable car up
10-min ascent, quietest and clearest car of the day. Confirm the September timetable.
8:30–11:30
Zugspitze summit
2,962m. Platforms, summit cross, Austrian terrace, and the Gletscherbahn down to the glacier plateau — all in the ticket.
~11:30 AM
Down, then drive into Partenkirchen
~15 km / 20 min. Park at P12 Rathausplatz, a few minutes from Ludwigstraße.
12:30–1:15
Walk Ludwigstraße and the old town
Painted Lüftlmalerei facades, Pfarrkirche St. Martin. Flat and easy after a morning of lifts.
1:15–2:30
Lunch — Gasthaus zur Schranne
Griesstraße 4. Augustiner on tap, beer garden if it holds. Book: 08821 9098030
~2:40 PM
Drive to Reutte, Austria
Griesen → B187 via Lermoos → B179. ~55 km, ~1h 5m. Toll- and vignette-free.
~3:45 PM
Park at the Ehrenberger Klause
Klause 1, 6600 Reutte. Marked exit right after the railway underpass. €5 for the day.
4:00–4:30
Walk up toward the bridge
20–30 min, ~100m of climb. Inclined lift available if you'd rather not.
4:30–5:05
Cross the highline179
406m of suspension bridge, 114m above the valley. €10 — machines are cash only, no €50 notes.
5:05–6:05
Burgruine Ehrenberg
13th-century ruin above the Fernpass road, free and largely unfenced. Best light of the day up here.
6:05–6:35
Walk back down to the Klause
Sunset ~7:10 PM, so no rush. Firm shoes matter on the descent.
~6:45 PM
Drive back to Munich
~2h via Füssen and the A96, or back through Garmisch on the A95. The Plansee/Linderhof route adds ~30 min and is worth it in daylight.

Book ahead: rental car and the timed Zugspitze slot (€78). Parking ~€7–10 at the Eibsee, €5 at Ehrenberg. Everything else is walk-up.
