Coffee, Cookies & Goodies
Island Vintage Coffee
🚶 7 min · Shops at Wailea

100% Kona coffee, poured by people who take it seriously. Order the Mauna Kea iced coffee: a cold-brew mountain of gelato, caramel and black Hawaiian sea salt. Add an açaí bowl and call it breakfast.

Morning line? It moves. The hotel's own Starbucks (0 min) is plan B.
Apple MapsGoogle Maps
Honolulu Cookie Company
🚶 7 min · Shops at Wailea

Buttery shortbread baked in the shape of tiny pineapples. Samples are free, which is dangerous. Grab a gift box of the pineapple macadamia and a chocolate-dipped Kona coffee. They fly home beautifully in a carry-on.

Apple MapsGoogle Maps
Island Gourmet Markets
🚶 7 min · Shops at Wailea

Part bakery, part deli, part treasure chest. Pastries are baked in-store daily. Fill a basket with Maui-grown coffee, macadamia nuts, local honey and lilikoʻi treats for the folks back home.

Jams, honey and butters count as liquids at security, so tuck them in a checked bag.

Wander & Wonder
Wailea Beach Path · North
🚶 10 min to Ulua Beach

A paved, palm-shaded ribbon along the sea. Head north to Ulua & Mōkapu, the quietest beaches, where turtles graze just offshore in the morning. Give honu at least 10 feet, as they're protected.

Apple MapsGoogle Maps
Grand Wailea gardens & art
🚶 12 min · south on the path

Wander through the gardens and open-air lobby of the Grand Wailea, home to a serious art collection that includes Fernando Botero's roly-poly bronze figures. Don't miss the little white seaside chapel. Free to stroll.

Apple MapsGoogle Maps
Four Seasons & Wailea Beach
🚶 20 min · south on the path

Keep going past the Grand Wailea for postcard views of Wailea Beach, with Kahoʻolawe and Molokini floating on the horizon. Turn around whenever your coffee runs out.
