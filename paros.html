Paros, Greece

Research Report — as of Sep 10, 2026

Paros is a Cyclades island between Mykonos and Santorini — whitewashed lanes, blue-domed churches, two very different main towns, and a reputation for being one of the strongest all-around Greek islands: good beaches, real inland villages, solid ferry links, and (per most sources) less overrun than its famous neighbors. It's also a world-class windsurfing/kitesurfing spot.

Trip is short: Sep 10–13 is only ~3 nights. Treat everything below as a menu to choose from, not a checklist — see Open Questions for a suggested day split.

Distances below are measured from the bullseye: Dimitrioi Alipranti 10, Paros 844 00 (Parikia, near the port). Under 30 min is shown as a walk time; over 30 min is shown as a drive distance. Left blank where a good estimate wasn't available.

Getting Around
  • Public bus (KTEL) — cheapest option. Hubs in Parikia and Naoussa; routes to Golden Beach, Piso Livadi, Aliki, Kolymbithres, the airport, and Pounta (Antiparos ferry). From €2.20/ride, €12 day pass. Service drops sharply after August — check schedules day-of.
  • Rental car — the better choice over a quad/ATV for a short visit ("95% of roads don't require a quad"); ~€30/day. Needed to reach Marathi quarries, Lefkes trailheads, and multiple beaches in one day.
  • Taxis — available at ports/main towns, pricier but simplest point-to-point; reserve ahead for transfers.
  • Walking — both Parikia and Naoussa old towns are pedestrian-friendly; parts of Naoussa aren't reachable by car at all.
  • Sea bus — seasonal boat from Parikia to north-shore beaches (Krios, Kolymbithres, Santa Maria); confirm September frequency.
  • To Antiparos — ferry from Pounta (not Parikia), ~7 min, every 30 min, ~€1.50/person cash. A vehicle is effectively required once there.
Tourist Spots / Must-See

First Order

Naoussa old harbor & Kastro (drive 9.6km)

Fishing boats, restored shipyards, boutiques and wine bars in the alleys — most-cited single "must walk around" spot on the island.

Panagia Ekatontapiliani Parikia (8 min)

"Church of 100 Doors" — one of the most important Byzantine monuments in Greece, 4th century AD, free to visit.

Frankish Castle & Old Market Parikia (5 min)

Pedestrian lanes and a Venetian-era castle built directly from recycled ancient marble blocks.

Kolymbithres Beach (drive 9km)

Sculpted granite boulders forming natural coves/pools — the island's most distinctive beach; great for families and snorkeling.

Lefkes (drive 11km)

Old inland capital, hill village, traditional architecture — "the best inland village," start/end of the Byzantine Road.

Byzantine Road (drive 11km to Lefkes trailhead)

3.5 km marble-paved path (Lefkes ↔ Prodromos), parts dating to ~1000 AD. Scenic, under 2 hrs one-way.

Second Order

  • Paros Archaeological Museum (Parikia) (9 min) — Cycladic figurines, a 470 BC Nike statue, mid-6th-c. BC marble Gorgo. Hours/price unconfirmed — verify locally.
  • Marpissa (drive 14km), Prodromos (drive 13km), Drios (drive 20km), Aliki (drive 12km) — smaller villages locals call "the real Paros" vs. Naoussa's more polished feel.
  • Antiparos day trip (drive 8km to Pounta, then ferry) — the Cave of Antiparos (411 steps, ~€6, ~1 hr) and the small intact Venetian Kastro. Realistically 6–8 hours, not a half-day.
  • Marathi ancient marble quarries (drive 5km) — source of marble for the Venus de Milo and Nike of Samothrace. Visit via scheduled free organized visits (no booking) or a private guide (~90 min–2 hrs). Confirm current dates.
Entertainment

First Order

  • Naoussa nightlife strip (drive 9.6km) — sunset/wine bars through late-night dance venues, concentrated in the old harbor alleys.
  • Moraitis Winery (Naoussa) (drive 11km) — third-generation family winery, tastings of native Parian varieties, small on-site historical exhibit.

Second Order

  • Paros Park (near Naoussa) (drive 12km) — protected headland, walking trails, sea caves, summer open-air cinema.
  • Boat trips to Despotiko and other islets — sold locally, weather-dependent (departure point varies).
Food & Beverage

Signature dishes & drinks — try these

  • Gouna — sun-dried salted mackerel/bonito grilled over charcoal; the island's signature dish, best with tsipouro or ouzo.
  • Kakavia — Parian fish soup, traditionally made with about a third seawater; among the best fish soups in Greece per multiple sources.
  • Revithada — slow-cooked chickpea stew, historically a Sunday dish baked communally.
  • Souma — the island's own grape-distilled spirit, made locally each October — ask for it by name instead of defaulting to ouzo.
  • Xinomizithra — tangy local cow's-milk cheese, shows up in pies, salads, and mezes.

Nature & Outdoors

First Order

  • Kolymbithres (drive 9km) — granite-cove swimming and snorkeling (see Sights above).
  • Mini Santa Maria Beach (drive 16km) — sheltered cove, exceptionally clear turquoise water; the beach to prioritize if you only get one.
  • Golden Beach (drive 18km) — the island's largest beach, major windsurfing/kitesurfing venue that hosts international competitions.
  • Byzantine Road hike (drive 11km to Lefkes trailhead) — the easy, scenic version of Paros hiking (see Sights above).

Second Order

  • Santa Maria Beach (drive 16km) — livelier, younger crowd, beach-club atmosphere (distinct from "Mini Santa Maria" — don't mix the two up).
  • Krios Beach — reachable by sea bus from Parikia, good all-rounder. (distance unconfirmed — short drive or boat taxi, verify locally)
  • Lefkes–Pigados–Marathi quarries hike (drive 11km to Lefkes trailhead) — 10 km/4 hrs one-way, or 14 km/5 hr loop; passes the 17th-c. Monastery of Agios Minas. Only realistic with a full day to spare.
  • Faragas Beach (drive 13km) — south-coast, rugged, sheltered from north winds, has a beach taverna.
Niche / Off-the-Beaten-Path
  • Lageri/Laggeri Beach (drive 13km) — undeveloped dune beach near Naousa with cedar trees; naturist-friendly, very few visitors even in peak season.
  • Piso Aliki Beach (drive 13km) — limestone south-coast shoreline, a local rather than tourist favorite, good seafood tavernas nearby.
  • Kalogeros Beach (drive 18km) — natural clay deposits locals apply as an informal spa treatment.
  • Monastiri Beach (drive 12km) — inside Paros Park, shallow and clear, with a path continuing to a quieter unorganized cove.
  • Marathi ancient marble quarries (drive 5km) — genuinely off-radar despite the history (marble for the Venus de Milo came from here).
  • Antiparos's channel-side beaches (Apantima, Soros) (drive 8km to Pounta, then ferry) — calm, current-protected, very light crowds even in August.
