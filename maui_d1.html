Twin Falls · Mile Marker 2

Your first taste of the rainforest. A short, easy trail leads through a working farm to a waterfall pool framed in ginger and bamboo.

Apple MapsGoogle Maps
Good to know: $10/car in the farm's paid lot (55 stalls). Last entry 4:30p. The lower falls are a ¼-mile walk; the upper falls ~1.4 mi round trip. Water shoes help. Closed to visitors the 1st Saturday of each month. Don't park on the highway.

Know Before You Go
  • Gas & cash. Fill up before Pāʻia, and carry small bills for fruit and banana-bread stands.
  • Parking is enforced. "No parking" zones along the highway carry fines and towing. Use only marked lots and pullouts.
  • Driving the road. Yield at one-lane bridges (take turns in small groups), and pull over to let locals pass.
  • Restrooms. Pāʻia, Twin Falls, Garden of Eden and Kaumahina Wayside.
  • Pack. Water shoes, swimwear, a light rain jacket, bug spray and reef-safe sunscreen.
  • Turnaround rule. Start back by 4:45p so you're off the narrow stretch before dark and at Hoʻokipa for sunset.
